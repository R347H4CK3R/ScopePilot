# Store listing draft

**Name:** ScopePilot
**Subtitle:** Evidence-led security research
**Short description:** Organize authorized research, inspect headers, and prepare clear report drafts.

## Description
Your best security research deserves a focused workspace.

ScopePilot brings program scope, response evidence, and report preparation together in a calm, beautifully organized mobile app. Track the programs you are authorized to research. Record exclusions and expiry dates. Inspect supplied HTTP response headers locally, then turn your observations into a draft you can review and share.

- Discover public community-listed HackerOne and Bugcrowd programs, then verify the official policy and scope.
- Keep official policies and scope close at hand.
- Import program records without silently inheriting testing permissions.
- Get evidence-led cookie, CORS, cache, CSP, and HSTS observations with explicit limitations and validation guidance.
- Redact common credential headers before saving.
- Maintain a local draft history and export using your device's share sheet.
- Work with the same focused interface on Android and iPhone.

Designed for authorized researchers. ScopePilot does not scan targets, execute exploits, automatically submit reports, or guarantee bounty eligibility or rewards. Missing headers alone do not establish vulnerabilities. Program rules and responsible disclosure requirements always apply.

## Business and release notes
Position as a focused research utility, not a guaranteed money-making agent. Pricing, subscription features, entitlements, support contact, legal entity, privacy-policy hosting, and store compliance have not been selected or implemented. Do not advertise paid features or automatic research that do not exist. This copy is a review draft, not a submitted listing.
